# Gap-fill pass 09 (hours first, then wifi)

WebSearch worked for the whole pass (about 25 queries, no limit reported). Venue sites were read with plain curl and WebFetch. I skipped nypl.org and instagram. Alternate NYPL hosts that showed up in search results (gopher.nypl.org, globallib.nypl.org) don't resolve, and libcal.nypl.org/hours shows only dashes for SNFL. Search summaries were not trusted on their own: one claimed a SoHo Grand page mentioned Wi-Fi at Bibliotheque, and when I fetched the page it didn't, so that claim wasn't used. Every source cited below was fetched and read.

6 of 16 records changed. 6 attributes went from unknown to known (3 hours, 3 wifi), and 1 wifi note was revised. No closures.

## Changed
- **verse-cafe-astoria**: hours unknown→Mon and Wed–Sun 7:00–18:00, closed Tue (low). The only listing is joe.coffee. An older directory (whereyoueat) shows Tuesday open, so this is in the note and gotcha #2 was updated. Source: https://joe.coffee/locations/ny/long-island-city/verse-long-island-city
- **capital-one-cafe-59th-lexington**: hours unknown→Mon–Fri 7:30–19:00, Sat–Sun 9:00–18:00 (low). AwardWallet (Aug 2026) gives 7:30am–7pm without naming days. TravelUpdate (Jul 2023, visit report) gives the weekday and weekend split. The official page still shows only banking hours. Gotcha #1 updated. Sources: https://awardwallet.com/credit-cards/capital-one-cafe/, https://travelupdate.com/deal-50-off-coffee-at-capital-one-cafes-for-cardholders/
- **public-hotel-lower-east-side**: hours unknown→Louis café 7:00–22:00 daily (low). The source is an undated Mr & Mrs Smith listing, which also gives the lobby bar as 11am–11pm. I revised the wifi note (value strong/low unchanged): Mr & Mrs Smith says free Wi-Fi throughout, but the hotel's own Eat & Drink page says non-guests get Wi-Fi with a paid Sun Club (rooftop) day pass, so free lobby Wi-Fi for non-guests is unclear. Sources: https://www.mrandmrssmith.com/luxury-hotels/public-new-york, https://www.publichotels.com/newyork/eat-and-drink
- **jefferson-market-library**, **nypl-battery-park-city**, **stavros-niarchos-foundation-library**: wifi unknown→ok (low). helpnewyork (accurate as of June 2026) says free "NYPL" Wi-Fi with no password or card at all NYPL locations during open hours. Speed isn't reported. Source: https://helpnewyork.com/free-wifi-nyc-2026-linknyc-parks-libraries-big-apple-connect/
