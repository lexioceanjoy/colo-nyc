# Batch 09 notes (2026-10-27)

Research constraints: the session's shared WebSearch budget ran out early in this batch, so most facts come from direct fetches of the candidate source URLs and the venues' own sites. nypl.org returned blocked/empty responses (Incapsula / 403) for every page, so NYPL hours and amenities could not be verified. The Fast Company 550 Madison article returned a 403, and the Lincoln Station and Kona websites could not be found.

## Spots
- devocion-downtown-brooklyn · Official site gives the address and hours (weekdays 7–6, weekends 7:30–6). Halfhalf (Sept 2026) says the free password Wi-Fi is turned off on weekends and the room is quiet with mostly people working. A Jan 2024 BK Mag piece says there are plenty of outlets. · Unknown: wifi quality (speed never reported), seating, timeLimit, calls, group, bookable, restroom. None of the sources address them.
- lincoln-station-prospect-heights · Halfhalf (Sept 2026) mentions perimeter tables plus a large communal table where laptop users sit, a "busy" interior, and opening until 9pm. · Unknown: hours (no official site found; listings conflict), wifi, outlets, timeLimit, calls, group (table size not stated), bookable, restroom.
- kona-coffee-east-village · NYU News (Mar 2025) and Working Line (Jun 2025) report plenty of outlets, lots of tables, a peaceful feel, limited weekend Wi-Fi, and filling up after lunch. · Unknown: hours (only "closes 9pm"), wifi quality, timeLimit, calls, bookable, restroom. The most recent proof that it is still open is from Jun 2025, so it needs human verification.
- capital-one-cafe-59th-lexington · The official page lists free Wi-Fi and outlets and nooks bookable by account holders. Halfhalf (2026) and WFHish (Feb 2025) describe lots of seating, call nooks, and good Wi-Fi. · Unknown: café hours (the official page lists only banking hours) and timeLimit. Restroom sources conflict (one customer bathroom vs "lots of bathrooms"), so restroom is low confidence.
- capital-one-cafe-union-square · The official page lists free Wi-Fi, outlets and bookable nooks. NYU News (Mar 2025) reports multiple floors, the most outlets in the city, no purchase needed, and Sunday crowds. · Unknown: café hours, wifi quality, noise, timeLimit, calls, group, restroom.
- sweetleaf-long-island-city · The official site confirms it is open at 10-93 Jackson Ave, Mon–Fri 7–7 and Sat–Sun 8–7. Atly (undated, branch not specified) mentions a quiet laptop room, free wifi, and no screens on weekends. Halfhalf calls the Jackson Ave cafe work-friendly but mislabels it as Astoria. · Unknown: wifi quality, outlets, seating, timeLimit, calls, group, bookable, restroom. The weekend rule is low confidence.
- the-hoxton-williamsburg · The official site lists the address, fast free Wi-Fi throughout, K'Far in the lobby, and Saturday 7–10pm jazz. Michelin and Metropolis Moving (both 2024) describe an expansive lobby with sofas and staff tolerant of remote workers. · Unknown: lobby hours, outlets, noise, timeLimit, calls, group, bookable, restroom. Purchase expectation is unconfirmed, so free=false.
- jefferson-market-library · Time Out (2022) covers the renovation, the 2nd-floor Adult Reading Room, rebuilt ADA restrooms, and improved power/data. Wikipedia gives the 425 Sixth Ave address. · Unknown: hours and all work attributes because nypl.org was blocked. Priority for a manual check.
- 550-madison-garden · APOPS (amenities current Mar 2023) lists 238 required seats, 104 tables, 2 restrooms, and a 7am–11pm covered space. 6sqft (Nov 2022) confirms 7–11 daily year-round, the glass canopy, public gender-inclusive restrooms, and coffee kiosks. · Unknown: wifi, outlets, noise, laptopPolicy, timeLimit, calls, bookable. The Snøhetta attribution was left out because the only source for it (Fast Company) returned a 403.
- nypl-library-for-the-performing-arts · The NYPL libguide confirms the address and entrances, the bag/coat check for the 3rd floor, and that reading-room hours differ from building hours. · Unknown: everything else, including hours and the laptop policy. nypl.org/locations/lpa and /lpa/research were blocked, so the candidate's "laptops allowed" claim is unverified. Priority for a manual check.

## Dropped
None. All 10 candidates showed evidence of still operating, though Kona's most recent evidence is Jun 2025, and none were paid coworking or laptop-banned.
