# Batch 05 notes (researched 2026-10-27)

Context: the shared WebSearch budget ran out early in this batch, and nypl.org, goop.com, varietycoffeeroasters.com and stonestreetcoffee.com refused or rate-limited fetches. As a result, hours are unknown for most cafes. All 10 candidates are kept: none showed closure evidence, but for several of them current open status rests only on recent guides or listings (flagged below). Recommend a second pass on hours once search is available.

- dweebs-bushwick · Halfhalftravel (updated Sep 2026) confirms Wi-Fi, roomy indoor seating and that it's laptop-welcome; BKMag Jan 2024 says Wi-Fi is fast and there are plenty of outlets (old, so low confidence). Unknown: hours (aggregator listings conflict: 7–5, 8–4, 8–5; no venue site found), noise, time limit, calls, group size, bookable space, restroom (no source).
- postmark-cafe-park-slope · Address corrected to **326 6th St** (the input said 6th Ave). The only work details are from BKMag Jan 2024 (quiet, plenty of seats and outlets, printer) and Your Brooklyn Guide Oct 2023, all low confidence. Laptop policy is low because two Your Brooklyn Guide pages conflict. Unknown: hours (2023 guide says 7:30/8–4; Atmosfy says 7–6 and closed Sunday; these conflict), time limit, calls, group size, bookable space, restroom. **Open status is weakly confirmed**: no closure reports found, but no 2025–26 source mentions it either.
- variety-coffee-chelsea · Halfhalftravel (Sep 2026) and itfeelsawesome.lol (Feb 2026) both report free Wi-Fi, small quartz tables, a busy all-day crowd and a restroom described as public; outlets come from HHT only. itfeelsawesome may draw on HHT, so it isn't treated as fully independent (confidence kept at medium). Unknown: hours (venue site returned 429), time limit, calls, group size, bookable space.
- mushtari-cafe-harlem · The Infatuation guide (Sep 2026) and its review (Feb 2025) describe good Wi-Fi, mostly metal stools, a plant-filled back area and a laptop-friendly feel. Unknown: hours (none found), outlets, noise (only "great playlists"), time limit, calls, group size, bookable space, restroom.
- stone-street-coffee-soho · The only detailed source is WFHish (Aug 2024, older than 18 months), so everything is low confidence: an 8-seat communal table, lots of plugs, OK Wi-Fi, fairly quiet, one restroom. Unknown: hours (site returned 429), laptop policy, time limit, calls, bookable space. **Open status not re-confirmed** for 2025–26. Stone Street opened a Southold café in 2026; don't use those hours.
- verse-cafe-astoria · The Infatuation (Sep 2026) and ATLY report Wi-Fi (ATLY says reliable), that it's best on weekdays and can get busy. Unknown: hours, outlets, noise, time limit, calls, group size, bookable space, restroom.
- brooklyn-public-library-central · The official BPL page gives hours (Mon–Thu 9–8, Fri–Sat 9–6, Sun 1–5) and bookable rooms (meeting rooms for 6–8 people, 2-person booths; adult card, 2-hour max, 2 bookings a month, 28 days ahead). Wikipedia gives the 5,500 sq ft Info Commons with laptop seating. Unknown: Wi-Fi and outlets (the BPL Wi-Fi page redirects off-domain and the brooklynbased "70+ laptops" source returned 401), noise, time limit, calls, restroom.
- oculus-world-trade-center · The official PANYNJ page lists free "!Free WTC WiFi" and public restroom locations and hours; The Tourist Checklist (May 2026) describes seating areas and busy times. Unknown: building hours (not on official page), outlets, noise, laptop policy, time limit, calls, group size, bookable space. The address uses the commonly listed 185 Greenwich St; PANYNJ uses 50 Church St for the WTC overall.
- nypl-53rd-street-library · Wikipedia gives the address (18 W 53rd St), the 2016 opening and a 10-stool laptop bar; Architectural Record (2016) describes the layout. nypl.org blocked the fetch (Incapsula), so hours, Wi-Fi, outlets, seating, noise, rooms and restroom are all unknown. Laptop policy is low (2016 data). Needs re-research from nypl.org.
- whole-foods-tribeca · The official Whole Foods store page gives hours of 7am–10pm daily, plus café seating and an in-store meeting space; a Tribeca Citizen post (Dec 2024) mentions some outlets, few isolated seats and that it can be very loud. Unknown: Wi-Fi (not mentioned anywhere), laptop policy, time limit, calls, group size, bookable space (the store lists a "meeting space", but public booking terms are unclear), restroom.
