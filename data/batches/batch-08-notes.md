# Batch 08 notes (2026-10-27)

Research caveat: the shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through this batch. After that, findings came only from direct fetches of the candidate source URLs and the venues' own sites. nypl.org returned blank or 403 pages, and an attempt to fetch it directly with curl was blocked. Several attributes are "unknown" for that reason and are worth a second pass once search is available again.

## Spots
- fontainhas-dumbo · Open; on The Infatuation's work-cafe guide (Sep 2026), which calls it "generally quiet and sunny". The venue's Square site shows a "Dinner Menu - 5pm to close", which is where the evening wine-bar gotcha comes from. · Unknown: hours (the site's JS didn't render), wifi, outlets, seating, calls, group, restroom, time limit, bookable space. No source covers them.
- sit-and-wonder-prospect-heights · Half Half (updated Sep 2026) lists it as laptop-friendly. Outlets come from Time Out (2010, "60 outlets") and BK Mag (Jan 2024), so confidence is low. Hours are 7am–8pm from the 2010 Time Out page, also low. No recent source confirms it is still open (search budget gone), so verify. · Unknown: wifi, seating, noise, calls, group, restroom, time limit.
- the-bean-east-village-2nd-ave · EV Grieve (Aug 2026): the Broadway and 3rd Ave Beans closed in 2026, and 54 2nd Ave is the last East Village location. thebeannyc.com is now a parked ad page. Half Half (Sep 2026) and itfeelsawesome (Feb 2026) say laptops are welcome. Atly (undated) says wifi sessions last 2h and renew with a purchase, and you need a purchase for the bathroom key. Hours conflict (7–21 vs 7–24), so they are left unknown. Note: The Working Line's "The Bean" entry is the now-closed 771 Broadway shop, so it is not cited. · Unknown: hours, noise, calls, group, bookable space.
- black-cat-les · WFHishNYC (Mar 2025) gives a seat inventory (5 two-tops, one 4-top, 3 stools, 6 couches), says it's calm, and says there's 1 customer bathroom. Wifi is reported reliable by WFHish and The Working Line (Jun 2025). Outlets conflict: "abundant" (WFHish) vs "limited" (TWL), so low. Printers come from itfeelsawesome. Hours are from Time Out (2017), so low. · Unknown: time limit, calls, bookable space.
- think-coffee-tribeca · WFHishNYC (Jan 2026): large space, about 15-seat communal table, outlets beside many tables, calls OK, one single bathroom. Wifi conflicts: WFHish says it works, but Half Half (Sep 2026) says Think locations have no wifi, so low. No time limit per The Infatuation (2023), low. · Unknown: hours (no official page fetched), bookable space, restroom access rule.
- overflow-coffee-long-island-city · Hours are 7am–6pm daily per the official site and QNS (Jan 2025). Fast wifi per The Infatuation guide (Sep 2026), Atly (Oct 2026) and The Infatuation's review (Feb 2025). There's a kids' play area in back. Atly's "takeaway only" line conflicts with everything else, so seating is low. · Unknown: noise level, calls, group, restroom, time limit, bookable space.
- williamsburgh-library · Official BPL page: hours (note Tue 1–8pm, Thu 10–8, Sat 10–5, closed Sun), fully accessible, meeting rooms bookable via bklynlibrary.org/rooms. · Unknown: wifi, outlets, seating, noise, laptop policy, calls, group, restroom. The branch page doesn't state these, and BPL amenity pages 404'd.
- 590-madison-atrium · APOPS (amenity data Mar 2023): 110 movable chairs, 32 movable tables, food kiosk, 8am–10pm, and up to 12 event closures a year. A 2024 APOPS comment says security stopped someone moving a table. Free wifi per Untapped (2021), low. Neighborhood set to midtown_east (Madison at 57th). · Unknown: outlets, noise, laptop policy, calls, restroom (not a required amenity), time limit.
