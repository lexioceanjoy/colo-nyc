# Batch 11 notes (2026-10-27)

Research constraints: the shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through. The nypl.org branch pages returned empty when fetched, and devocion.com, partnerscoffee.com and varietycoffeeroasters.com all returned HTTP 429. As a result, the library hours and several chain-cafe hours are unknown.

- center-for-fiction-fort-greene · The official visit page (seen as a search snippet) says the Café & Bar is open 9am–8pm daily, but Half Half (Sep 2026) says 11am–8pm, so hours are low-confidence with a note. Indoor seating closes around 5–5:30pm on event nights. WFHishNYC (Apr 2025) reports 8-seat communal tables, about 4 outlets, a 3-hour Wi-Fi cap with purchase and a noisy high ceiling, while Half Half and BK Mag call it quiet, so noise is low-confidence. · Unknown: callsOk, bookableSpace (no sources).
- devocion-williamsburg · **Address conflict:** Half Half (2026) and Domino Park (2024) say 69 Grand St, but The Infatuation (2017 review and 2026 guide), a Toast order URL and helpnewyork say 148 Grand St. I used 69 Grand St and added a gotcha; this needs checking at the Places resolve step. Hours and 180 Mb/s Wi-Fi come from helpnewyork (May 2026). Weekend Wi-Fi shutoff appears only in older or indirect sources, so laptopPolicy is "welcome" at low confidence. · Unknown: timeLimitMin, groupCapacity, bookableSpace, restroom.

- qahwah-house-morningside-heights · Hours come from Acolyte Living (Jul 2026) and Barnard Bulletin (2024), which agree on closing times. West Side Rag (Nov 2025) describes two floors of tables.
