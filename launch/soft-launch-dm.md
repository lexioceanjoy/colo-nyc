# Soft-launch DM (DRAFT, for 5–10 friends before the public posts)

Goal: each friend adds or fills in their **3 regular spots**, so the map is fuller (and the visitor-report features are battle-tested) before the Next Play / X / 小红书 posts.

## English
> Hey! I built a little map of good places to work in NYC: **colo-nyc.vercel.app** (and you can see who's working where today).
>
> Small favor before I share it widely: could you add or fill in your **3 regular work spots**? Takes ~3 minutes:
> 1. Search or tap 📍 Near me. If your spot's there, open it and tap "Know this?" on anything that says Unknown (Wi-Fi, outlets, noise, laptop rules, hours)
> 2. If it's not there, tap "+ Suggest a spot" and add it
>
> You'll need to sign in with Google so your answers count. Anything that looks wrong? Tell me. Thank you!! 🙏

## 中文
> 嗨！我做了一个纽约办公地图：**colo-nyc.vercel.app**（还能看到今天谁在哪儿办公）
>
> 正式发之前想请你帮个小忙：把你**最常去的 3 个办公地点**加进来或者补全一下，大概 3 分钟：
> 1. 搜一下或者点「📍 附近」，找到的话点进去，把显示「暂不清楚」的项（Wi-Fi、插座、安静度、能否开电脑、营业时间）点「你知道吗？」补上
> 2. 没找到的话，点「+ 推荐地点」加进去
>
> 需要用 Google 登录，你的回答才会算数。看到哪里不对也直接告诉我～谢谢！！🙏
